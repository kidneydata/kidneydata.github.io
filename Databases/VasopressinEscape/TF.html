RNA-Seq in Microdissected Rat Cortical Collecting Ducts

Changes in Transcript Abundances during Onset of Vasopressin Escape: Transcription Factors

Description: This page reports data from RNA-Seq analysis of microdissected renal cortical collecting ducts from rats during onset of vasopressin escape versus time-controls. The values are calculated from FPKM values from escape versus control pairs on Day 1 (3 pairs), Day 2 (4 pairs) and Day 4 (3 pairs) after initiation of water loading in a rat model of SIADH (syndrome of inappropriate antidiuresis). Log2(Escape/Control) values are mean values over all replicates for each day. Values with t-test-derived Benjamini-Hochberg FDR-adjusted P values(Padj) less than 0.1 are highlighted in color: Dark green, increased with Padj<0.05; Light green, increased with 0.05< Padj<0.1; Dark red, decreased with Padj<0.05; Light red, decreased with 0.05< Padj<0.1. See link for web page reporting all data. Transcript Abundance was calculated from FPKM values in control rats. Abundance values were re-normalized to the value at Rank 4000 for each time point (Value presented is mean of Day1, Day 2 and Day 4 values.). Values are highlighted in shades of yellow according to relative abundance. Database created by Jae Wook Lee and Mark Knepper. Please address questions to knep@helix.nih.gov. Download data here.
